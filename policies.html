4. Customer Accounts & Order Data

This website offers a free customer account system used for Signal Fashion orders and for reading full-length articles past their free preview. This section explains what we collect through accounts and how it's handled.

Creating an account

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. You may optionally provide your full name, phone number, and topic interests to help us understand what content and products you'd find useful. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are cryptographically hashed (PBKDF2 with a unique salt per account) before being saved, meaning even we cannot view your actual password.

Signing in and sessions

When you sign in, a secure session token is stored as a cookie in your browser so you don't need to log in on every page. If you select "Remember me," this session lasts up to 30 days. If you do not select it, the session ends when you close your browser. You can sign out at any time from your account page, which clears this session immediately.

Order history and shipping details

When you place a Signal Fashion order while signed in, we store your order details — product, size, color, price, and the shipping address you provide — so you can view your past orders from your account page and so we can fulfil and reference your order if you contact us about it. This data is retained as part of our standard business and tax record-keeping.

Incomplete or abandoned orders

If you begin the checkout process — including selecting a product, entering shipping details, or reaching the payment step — but do not complete payment, we may receive a notification containing whatever information you had already entered (such as your name, email, or the product you were viewing). This helps us identify and resolve technical issues with checkout, and occasionally to follow up directly if it looks like you ran into a problem completing your purchase, including offering assistance or a discount. If you do not wish to be contacted about an incomplete order, you can let us know at contact@vishalhingolauthor.com and we will not follow up on future incomplete orders tied to your email.

Free preview / account-gated articles

Some longer articles on this site show roughly the first 30% of the content for free to all visitors, with the remainder visible only to visitors who create a free account or sign in. This is a content access feature, not a paywall — accounts are free and creating one does not involve any payment. The full text of every article remains present in the page itself for search engines and AI tools, so this feature does not affect how the content is indexed or discovered.

Editing or deleting your account

You can update your name, phone number, and topic interests at any time from your account page. You can permanently delete your account at any time from your account page. When you delete your account, your login credentials and saved preferences are removed immediately. Past order records are retained (with the account link removed) for our business and tax record-keeping obligations, consistent with the order-history retention described above. To request complete removal of historical order data beyond what we are legally required to retain, email contact@vishalhingolauthor.com.

Where account data is stored

Account, session, and order data is stored in Cloudflare D1, a database service operated by Cloudflare, the same infrastructure provider used for this website's security and content delivery (see Section 5).
