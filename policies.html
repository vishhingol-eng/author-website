2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Summary: Pre-session cancellations receive an 80% refund. Post-session refunds are 50% for single sessions. Monthly plan refunds are calculated based on sessions attended.

Single session ($29) — cancellation

  • Before the session takes place: You will receive an 80% refund ($23.20). The remaining 20% covers non-recoverable payment gateway fees and administrative costs.
  • After a completed session: If you feel the session did not meet your expectations, you may request a 50% refund ($14.50) within 24 hours of the session. Requests must be submitted via email to contact@vishalhingolauthor.com.
  • After 24 hours of a completed session: No refund is available.

Monthly mentorship ($99/month) — cancellation

You may cancel your monthly mentorship at any time. Refunds for the current billing cycle are calculated based on the number of sessions attended, charged at the single-session rate ($29 per session):

  • 0 sessions attended: 80% refund ($79.20)
  • 1 session attended: $29 deducted, $70 refunded
  • 2 sessions attended: $58 deducted ($29 x 2), $41 refunded
  • 3 sessions attended: $12 refunded
  • 4 sessions attended: No refund — all sessions have been used

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No further charges will be applied after cancellation.

How to request a refund

Send an email to contact@vishalhingolauthor.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

  • Your full name

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method.
