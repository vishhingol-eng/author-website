You Don't Attract What You Want. You Attract What You Carry.

The uncomfortable truth about why like attracts like — and it has nothing to do with magic.

Vishal Hingol Jul 2026 8 min read
✦Frequency

The law of attraction, as popularly understood, suggests that thinking positive thoughts will attract positive outcomes into your life. This is an oversimplification so extreme that it has done more damage than good. You do not attract what you think. You attract what you are — and what you are is determined by the emotional frequency you carry most consistently, not the thoughts you perform during a morning journal session.

Emotional frequency is not a mystical concept. It is a description of your default emotional state — the baseline your nervous system returns to when you are not consciously performing anything. Some people's baseline is anxiety. Some people's baseline is curiosity. Some people's baseline is quiet confidence. This baseline is what other people sense when they meet you, what opportunities either gravitate toward or away from, and what your Reticular Activating System uses to filter which parts of reality you notice.

The Confirmation Loop

Your RAS filters 11 million bits of sensory data per second down to approximately 40 bits of conscious experience. The filter criteria is not random — it is calibrated by what your emotional baseline has declared to be important and true. If your baseline is scarcity, your RAS surfaces evidence of scarcity everywhere. If your baseline is possibility, your RAS surfaces evidence of possibility everywhere. Same world. Same data. Completely different conscious experience.

This is not magical thinking. This is attentional neuroscience. The world is not changing to match your frequency — your perception of the world is changing, which changes what you notice, which changes what you act on, which changes your actual outcomes. The mechanism is entirely natural. The results feel supernatural because the process is invisible.

How to Shift Your Baseline

You do not shift your baseline by thinking differently. You shift it by creating experiences that provide your nervous system with new evidence. One genuine act of generosity shifts your baseline more than a hundred affirmations about abundance. One conversation where you speak honestly shifts your baseline more than a month of journaling about authenticity. The nervous system trusts experience. It does not trust performance.

The takeaway

Stop trying to think your way into a new frequency. Create one small experience today that gives your nervous system evidence of the person you are becoming. Your RAS will do the rest — not because it is magic, but because that is literally what it was built to do.
