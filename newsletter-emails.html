THE SIGNAL · EDITION #02

The 369 Method Nobody Talks About

Hi there,

The 369 method went viral on TikTok. Millions tried it. Most quit within a week.

Here's why: they were doing the surface version.

Writing an affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon, 9 at night — that's the structure. But structure without understanding is just ritual, and ritual without frequency is just habit.

The real 369 method works because of three principles nobody explains:

1. The 3 is not repetition — it's declaration. Your morning write is a statement of identity. Not "I want money." Rather: "I am someone who receives abundance naturally."

2. The 6 is not reinforcement — it's embodiment. Your afternoon write is done while feeling the emotion of already having it. If you can't feel it, you're not ready for it — and that's useful information.

3. The 9 is not completion — it's surrender. Your evening write releases attachment to the outcome. You've stated it, felt it, and now you let it go. The universe doesn't respond to desperation. It responds to frequency.

I built an entire 90-day guided journal around this deeper version of the method — day by day, with prompts designed to take you through the real transformation.

THE SIGNAL · EDITION #03

Your Phone Is Stealing Your Signal

Hi there,

Let me ask you something: what was the first thing you did when you woke up this morning?

If you reached for your phone before your feet touched the floor — you just gave away the most powerful 20 minutes of your entire day.

The first 20 minutes after waking, your brain is in a theta-to-alpha state — the same state meditators spend years trying to access. In those minutes, whatever you consume goes directly into your subconscious without filtering.

News headlines. Social media anxiety. Someone else's curated life. That's what most people feed their subconscious every single morning.

Digital sovereignty isn't about deleting apps or going off-grid. It's about three specific boundaries:

  • First 60 minutes: No screen. Journal, walk, breathe, or sit in silence.
  • Input diet: For every hour of consumption, create for 30 minutes.
  • Evening cutoff: No news or social media after 9 PM. Your sleep quality is your subconscious processing time.

I wrote Digital Sovereignty as a complete guide to reclaiming your attention — the most valuable resource you own.

THE SIGNAL · EDITION #05

The Architecture of Your Next Year

Hi there,

Every December, I sit down with a blank notebook and do something most people skip entirely: I design the architecture of the year ahead.

Not goals. Not resolutions. Architecture.

Here's the difference: a goal is a destination. Architecture is the building itself — the daily structure, the weekly rhythms, the monthly checkpoints that make the goal inevitable rather than aspirational.

My framework has four layers:

Layer 1: Identity. Who do you need to become? Not what do you want to achieve — who do you need to be for those achievements to be natural? Write 3 identity statements.

Layer 2: Systems. What daily systems does that person operate? Morning routine. Evening wind-down. Weekly review. Monthly deep work day. Design these before January 1.

Layer 3: Environment. What needs to change in your physical space, your digital space, and your social circle to support those systems? Remove friction before it appears.

Layer 4: Accountability. Who will you share this with? Not for motivation — for honest feedback. One person who will tell you when you're drifting.

This framework is the backbone of The Architecture of Destiny — a novel about a man who discovers that destiny isn't found, it's built.
