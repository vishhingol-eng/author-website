// About the Author

Hi, I'm Vishal.

I write self-help books and structured guided journals for readers across India and the world. My work sits at the meeting point of practical psychology and quiet daily ritual — less hype, more pages that actually get filled in.

Every journal I publish is built around one belief: change doesn't come from motivation, it comes from evidence. Write the proof down every day, and the mind has no choice but to follow.

All of my books are available worldwide on Amazon, in Kindle and paperback editions.
