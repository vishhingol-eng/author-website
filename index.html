// About the Author

The signal has always been transmitting.

Vishal Hingol writes at the intersection of human psychology, personal transformation, and the mechanics by which people build lives of genuine meaning and abundance.

His work is grounded in a single sustained conviction: the gap between where most people are and where they are capable of being is not a gap of effort, intelligence or opportunity. It is a gap of frequency — and frequency can be learned.

Zara and the Divine Signal is his most ambitious work to date: a complete human journey, told through one person over twenty-three years, showing through lived experience how the signal works, what the noise costs, and what becomes possible when you choose to transmit what you actually are.

Every person alive is carrying a signal the world needs. The work is not to acquire a new one — it is to clear the interference from the one you were born with. This work is an invitation to receive it.

— Vishal

Vishal Hingol is a self-help author and mindset coach from Surat, Gujarat, India. He writes books on mindset, manifestation, the 369 method, personal transformation, self-sabotage, focus, and intentional living. His books are available on Amazon as Kindle eBooks and paperbacks worldwide. Topics include the law of attraction, neuroscience, guided journals, and breaking self-limiting beliefs.
