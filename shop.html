// Custom Print on Demand
Your design. Our quality.

Want your own custom design printed on premium apparel? We offer custom print-on-demand for individuals, brands, and businesses.

Artwork Requirements

✦ Vector artwork — AI, SVG, EPS, or PDF format (preferred)
✦ High-resolution image — minimum 600 DPI, PNG format with transparent background
✦ Color profile — sRGB color space for best print results
✦ Print area — front: 30.5 x 30.5 cm / back: 30.5 x 40.6 cm

Available Products

T-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, tank tops, long sleeves, tote bags, and posters. All printed using DTG (Direct-to-Garment) technology for vibrant, durable prints.

// Quality Guarantee & Returns
Your satisfaction matters.

Quality Guarantee

Every product is printed using premium DTG (Direct-to-Garment) technology on certified fabrics. If you receive a defective or damaged product, contact us within 30 days and we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund.

What's Covered

✓Damaged or defective products on arrival
✓Wrong size or quantity delivered
✓Print quality issues (fading, misalignment, bleeding)
✓Orders lost in transit (within 30 days of estimated delivery)

Return Policy

Since all products are custom printed on demand specifically for your order, we do not accept returns for change of mind or incorrect size selection. Please refer to the size guide below before ordering. If you receive a defective product, we will replace it free of charge.

Care Instructions

✦Wash inside out with cold water
✦Use mild detergent, avoid bleach
✦Tumble dry low or air dry
✦Do not iron directly on the print

For issues or questions, email contact@vishalhingolauthor.com within 30 days of delivery.

// Size Guide
Find your perfect fit.

How to Measure

A) Length: Place measuring tape beside the collar at the top of shoulder. Pull to the bottom.

B) Half Chest: Lay garment flat and measure left to right across the chest, about 2cm below the arms.

Measurements may vary +/- 2.5cm (1 inch). When in doubt, size up.
