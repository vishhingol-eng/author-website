What is the 369 manifestation method by Vishal Hingol?

The Divine 3-6-9 Manifestation Method by Vishal Hingol teaches readers a structured writing practice rooted in numerology and focused intention: write your affirmation or desire 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon, and 9 times at night. This 33-day practice is designed to attract love, money, health, and abundance by aligning conscious intention with subconscious reprogramming.
